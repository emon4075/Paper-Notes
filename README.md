# Paper Notes

A running log of the papers I read, one markdown note per paper. I keep it
because a paper I can't summarize in a page is a paper I didn't actually read —
the template forces me to name the gap the work fills, the number that carries
the claim, and the parts I still don't understand. It's public so the notes stay
honest and so anyone can see what I've been reading.

## Areas

- **`ml`** — architectures, training and optimization, representation learning.
- **`nlp`** — language models, prompting, evaluation, alignment.
- **`security`** — cryptography, attacks and defenses, privacy-preserving methods.

## How I read

Every note is tagged `deep` or `skim`, and the split is a budget decision, not a
quality judgment.

**Skim** is one pass: abstract, figures, results table, conclusion. I want the
claim, the shape of the method, and the headline number — enough to know whether
the paper matters to me and enough to cite it without misrepresenting it. The
"unsure about" section on a skim is usually long, and that's the honest outcome.

**Deep** means I work through the method until I could re-derive or reimplement
it, check whether the experiments actually support the claim (baselines, ablations,
what's conspicuously not measured), and chase the references that the argument
leans on. Every heading in the template gets filled in properly.

Most notes are skims. I reserve deeps for papers I'm building on or arguing with.

## Adding a note

1. Copy `templates/note.md` to `notes/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`.
2. Fill in the frontmatter and the eight headings.
3. Run `python scripts/update_index.py` to rebuild the table above.
